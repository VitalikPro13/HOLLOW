# Call surfaces (`lib/src/ui/call/`) -- one stage for DM calls, voice rooms and meetings

Built 2026-09-25 (design language session 22) from the approved session 21 mockup and the build brief (`tmp3.txt`). Desktop only so far; the phone screens are session 23.

## The decisions it encodes

- **One surface (D1).** DM call, voice room and conference meeting all render `CallStage` + `CallStageBar`; only the adapter differs.
- **Chat-first DM (D2).** A DM voice call is a 56 px `DmCallRow` under the header; the conversation stays the page. The stage replaces the message list when a camera is on (either side), a share is watched or sent, or the user presses "Open the call" (`dmStageOpenedProvider` holds that call id). "Back to the chat" sits in the bar's layout slot only while nothing forces the stage. While the stage is up the DM's ONE right panel is Chat (the same `_buildMessageArea`, 320 wide, `surface`) or Profile, swapped from the header.
- **A voice room you are in is always the stage (D3)**, the channel chat (`ChannelChatPane(headerTitle: 'Chat')`) in a 300 px side panel (`vcChatPanelOpenProvider`, session-sticky). The shell hides the member panel whenever a voice channel is selected: one side panel. Joining SELECTS the room (event_provider `VoiceChannelJoined`) and leaving restores the previous channel, so the "room you are not in" preview (tiles + one filled "Join voice", or "Nobody's here yet") is an edge state.
- **Speaking = `SpeakingRing`** in the person's own `nameColorFor(master)`, yours the accent fill; paints OUTSIDE its child with a gap (2/2, dense 1.5/1.5, large 3/3), fades via `AnimatedContainer` from the ring colour at alpha 0, no glow. Muted never rings. The old `SpeakingBorder` / `SpeakingOverlayRing` in `components/speaking_border.dart` survive ONLY for the phone screens until session 23.
- **Focus only moves on a click (D7).** `resolveStageFocus(requested, live, liveShares)`: a requested source that is live stands; one that ended falls to the next live share, else nothing (the grid); nothing requested stays nothing. A camera turning on, an offer or a watcher never changes it. Adapters write the resolved focus BACK post-frame (`_normalizeFocus`), so an ended camera does not reclaim focus when it returns. Watch focuses its share; starting your own share focuses it only when nothing else is focused (DM in `toggleDmScreenShare`, VC in the provider's `startScreenShare`). Provider `stopWatchingScreenShare` no longer falls back to a camera.
- **Strip on top (D8).** A focused source is a 16:9 box; above it the people strip (132x76 tiles, non-focused shares first, the focused camera's owner left out) in an `EdgeScrollRow`, collapsible (`callStripCollapsedProvider`); collapsed, a line says who is speaking. The chevron keeps the RIGHT end in both states ("Hide the people" / "Show the people", test-pinned).
- **Everything on the bar (D9), always visible (D10).** Order: mono timer (h:mm:ss past an hour, `CallDurationText.format`) + recording mark | Mute (`CallMuteButton`, PTT-aware) Deafen | Camera Share | Layout ("Show everyone" / "Focus the screen" / DM "Back to the chat") Full screen More | red `CallLeaveButton` ("Leave the call" / "Leave the room" / "Leave the meeting"). Muted/deafened = error 14% fill (`HollowIconButton.fill`), on = grey `selected`. More: Record the call (Windows, macOS), the share audio panel while watching, Audio and video settings.
- **Fullscreen (D11).** Clicking the focused share (double-click a focused camera) pushes `CallFullscreenRoute` on the root navigator; it enters the window fullscreen through `fullscreenProvider` via the `FullscreenMediaChrome` mixin (a user already in F11 stays there). Strip, bar and the share's own labels fade after 2 s idle (Timer, no Ticker), never while hovered or focused, back on any move or key; Escape pops the route; Reduce motion keeps them visible. Double-click is a raw `DoubleClickListener`, never an arena recognizer (it would delay every bar click).

## Files

- `call_stage_data.dart` -- `CallSourceId`, `CallPerson` (DEVICE id for tiles, master for names/colours, `speaking` and `link` as `ProviderListenable`s so a flip rebuilds one tile), `CallShare` (offer until watched; `watchers` as masters), `CallStageData`, `resolveStageFocus`, `stageGridColumns` (1-2 -> n, 3-4 -> 2, 5-9 -> 3, 10+ -> 4).
- `call_stage_sources.dart` -- `DmCallStageSource(peerMaster)` and `VcCallStageSource(serverId, channelId)`; `isDmCallWith` (an incoming call's `peerId` is the caller's DEVICE, outgoing the MASTER: always compare through the resolver), `dmStageForced`, `watchDmStageShown`, `dmCallPeerName`, the DM peer volume menu.
- `call_stage.dart` -- `CallStage` (pane and fullscreen), `StageGrid`, `openCallFullscreen`, `CallFullscreenRoute`.
- `call_person_tile.dart` (large / strip / compact), `share_tile.dart` (+ `CallScrimButton`), `call_stage_bar.dart` (+ `CallToggleButton`, `CallMuteButton`, `CallLeaveButton`), `dm_call_row.dart`, `call_actions.dart` (`startDmCallFlow` = THE start path: TURN check, leave-the-room confirm, opens the DM; `leaveDmCall`; the two share toggles), `call_theme.dart` (`callMediaTheme` = dark-ladder colours for text over video in both themes, `CallMediaLabel`, `CallMetrics`), `call_recording_toasts.dart` (the saved/failed toasts ActiveCallBar used to host).

## Presence elsewhere (5.5)

Dock: `VoiceQuickControls` handles DM calls too (Cancel while ringing out, else mute/deafen/Leave) and `DockIdentity`'s line reads "Calling X" / "In a call with X" (opens the DM). Classic: `VoiceChannelPanel` is the "you" block for a voice room OR a DM call (no dock there). Header: the friend chip carries `VoiceHereBadge(icon: phone)`, "in a call with you" in its label. Home: `ConversationRow.live` shows "In a call · 04:12". The incoming card (`dialogs/incoming_call_dialog.dart`) is 340 wide under the header, ghost Decline + filled Accept (Accept opens the DM on desktop), a 2 px countdown line stepped by the 1 s Timer.

## Provider additions (Dart only, no wire change)

`CallState.peerWatchingMyShare` (sharer side of `screen_watch`), `CallNotifier.remoteVolume`, `VoiceChannelState.shareWatchers` (mirrors the private `_watchers` via `_syncWatchers()` at every mutation), `VoiceChannelNotifier.clearFocus()`.
