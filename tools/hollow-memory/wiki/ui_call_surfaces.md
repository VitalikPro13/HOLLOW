# Call surfaces (`lib/src/ui/call/`) -- one stage for DM calls, voice rooms and meetings

Built 2026-09-25 from the approved session 21 mockup and the build brief (`tmp3.txt`): desktop in session 22, the phone and the DM call record line in session 23.

## The decisions it encodes

- **One surface (D1).** DM call, voice room and conference meeting all render `CallStage` + `CallStageBar`; only the adapter differs.
- **Chat-first DM (D2).** A DM voice call is a 56 px `DmCallRow` under the header; the conversation stays the page. The stage replaces the message list when a camera is on (either side), a share is watched or sent, or the user presses "Open the call" (`dmStageOpenedProvider` holds that call id). "Back to the chat" sits in the bar's layout slot only while nothing forces the stage. While the stage is up the DM's ONE right panel is Chat (the same `_buildMessageArea`, framed by `CallStageWithPanel`: resizable, the width shared with voice channels and meetings, see wiki ui_voice_channel_pane) or Profile, swapped from the header.
- **A voice room you are in is always the stage (D3)**, the channel chat (`ChannelChatPane(headerTitle: 'Chat')`) in the resizable `CallStageWithPanel` side panel (`vcChatPanelOpenProvider`, session-sticky; 300 to 560, one saved width for every call surface). The shell hides the member panel whenever a voice channel is selected: one side panel. Joining SELECTS the room (event_provider `VoiceChannelJoined`) and leaving restores the previous channel, so the "room you are not in" preview (tiles + one filled "Join voice", or "Nobody's here yet") is an edge state.
- **Speaking = `SpeakingRing`** in the person's own name colour (`callRingColor(.., field:)`: a chosen colour's FIRST stop, since a ring and a call label draw one colour; else the hashed one), yours the accent fill unless you chose a colour; paints OUTSIDE its child with a gap (2/2, dense 1.5/1.5, large 3/3), fades via `AnimatedContainer` from the ring colour at alpha 0, no glow. Muted never rings. `components/speaking_border.dart` is deleted (session 23): the phone uses this ring too.
- **Focus only moves on a click (D7).** `resolveStageFocus(requested, live, liveShares)`: a requested source that is live stands; one that ended falls to the next live share, else nothing (the grid); nothing requested stays nothing. A camera turning on, an offer or a watcher never changes it. Adapters write the resolved focus BACK post-frame (`_normalizeFocus`), so an ended camera does not reclaim focus when it returns. Watch focuses its share; starting your own share focuses it only when nothing else is focused (DM in `toggleDmScreenShare`, VC in the provider's `startScreenShare`). Provider `stopWatchingScreenShare` no longer falls back to a camera.
- **Strip on top (D8).** A focused source is a 16:9 box; above it the people strip (132x76 tiles, non-focused shares first, the focused camera's owner left out) in an `EdgeScrollRow`, collapsible (`callStripCollapsedProvider`); collapsed, a line says who is speaking. The chevron keeps the RIGHT end in both states ("Hide the people" / "Show the people", test-pinned).
- **Everything on the bar (D9), always visible (D10).** Order: mono timer (h:mm:ss past an hour, `CallDurationText.format`) + recording mark | Mute (`CallMuteButton`, PTT-aware) Deafen | Camera Share | Layout ("Show everyone" / "Focus the screen" / DM "Back to the chat") Full screen More | red `CallLeaveButton` ("Leave the call" / "Leave the room" / "Leave the meeting"). Muted/deafened = error 14% fill (`HollowIconButton.fill`), on = grey `selected`. More: Record the call (Windows, macOS), the share audio panel while watching, Audio and video settings.
- **Fullscreen (D11).** Clicking the focused share (double-click a focused camera) pushes `CallFullscreenRoute` on the root navigator; it enters the window fullscreen through `fullscreenProvider` via the `FullscreenMediaChrome` mixin (a user already in F11 stays there). Strip, bar and the share's own labels fade after 2 s idle (Timer, no Ticker), never while hovered or focused, back on any move or key; Escape pops the route; Reduce motion keeps them visible. Double-click is a raw `DoubleClickListener`, never an arena recognizer (it would delay every bar click). **Your own share never goes full screen** (Vitalik 11-14: it would film the screen showing it, a hall of mirrors): focused, its click does nothing and the bar drops Full screen (`watchBar` gets a null `onFullscreen`); inside the full screen its strip and grid tiles take no click and draw the placeholder, never the live preview (`_withoutPreview`; the pane grid keeps it), and should the focus land on it any other way (Layout, a watched share ending) the route closes back to the pane (`_ownShareFocused`, branching on `CallShare.isMine`, never an id compare). Someone else's share is unchanged. `test/widget/own_share_fullscreen_test.dart` (DM, room and meeting sources, plus the phone).

## Files

- `call_stage_data.dart` -- `CallSourceId`, `CallPerson` (DEVICE id for tiles, master for names/colours, `speaking` and `link` as `ProviderListenable`s so a flip rebuilds one tile), `CallShare` (offer until watched; `watchers` as masters), `CallStageData`, `resolveStageFocus`, `stageGridColumns` (1-2 -> n, 3-4 -> 2, 5-9 -> 3, 10+ -> 4).
- `call_stage_sources.dart` -- `DmCallStageSource(peerMaster)` and `VcCallStageSource(serverId, channelId)`; `isDmCallWith` (an incoming call's `peerId` is the caller's DEVICE, outgoing the MASTER: always compare through the resolver), `dmStageForced`, `watchDmStageShown`, `dmCallPeerName`, the DM peer volume menu.
- `call_stage.dart` -- `CallStage` (pane and fullscreen), `StageGrid`, `openCallFullscreen`, `CallFullscreenRoute`.
- `call_person_tile.dart` (large / strip / compact), `share_tile.dart` (+ `CallScrimButton`), `call_stage_bar.dart` (+ `CallToggleButton`, `CallMuteButton`, `CallLeaveButton`), `dm_call_row.dart`, `call_actions.dart` (`startDmCallFlow` = THE start path: TURN check, leave-the-room confirm, opens the DM; `leaveDmCall`; the two share toggles), `call_theme.dart` (`callMediaTheme` = dark-ladder colours for text over video in both themes, `CallMediaLabel`, `CallMetrics`), `call_recording_toasts.dart` (the saved/failed toasts ActiveCallBar used to host).

## Phone (session 23, `lib/src/ui/mobile/`)

The same adapters feed the phone: `MobileCallScreen` builds `DmCallStageSource(master)`, `MobileVoiceChannelRoute` a `VcCallStageSource`, so people, shares, focus, watch and stop all come from the one data model. Shared phone pieces are in `mobile_call_chrome.dart`.

- **One top bar** (`MobileCallTopBar`, 56): "Minimise the call" chevron, the title over a mono subtitle (DM: Ringing / Connecting / the timer; room: "Synth Lab · 42:17", a meeting just the timer), "Open the chat" (`openMobileCallChat`: pops back to the chat under the call, or opens it; a room's chat opens through `openMobileChannelOn`, so it lands on its server page, wiki `ui_mobile` "Server page"; none for a meeting).
- **One control row** (`MobileCallControlRow` + `MobileCallControl`): see wiki `ui_mobile`. Speaker = `speakerControl`, which toggles through the providers' `preferLoudRoute` (headset beats speaker) and long-presses to the route sheet. Share on a phone = the OS sheet (`toggleMobileShare`), a narrow desktop window keeps the desktop picker. `isPhoneCallPlatform` reads `defaultTargetPlatform`, so renders can show the phone set.
- **DM audio:** the other person's 132 avatar with `SpeakingRing.large` in their colour, the name in `heading` in that colour, a badge naming the link's state only while it is not healthy ("Unstable connection", "Reconnecting", "Connection lost", from `LinkHealthSnapshot.label`; never a catch-all), the offer strip ("Sharing their screen" + Watch) or your own share card under it; you at 56 top-right, "You" in the accent. **DM video:** their camera as a large `CallPersonTile` (ring and marks on the frame), yours a draggable 90x120 strip tile; the offer stays reachable over the video.
- **Room:** offers as full-width cards (`MobileShareOffer`), your share card (`MobileOwnShare`, never previewed), then `MobileRoomGrid`: two columns of 128 tall `CallPersonTile`s (avatar 56 via the new `avatarSize`), a camera fills its person's tile (nobody drops out), long press = the person's menu.
- **Watching** (`MobileWatchingView`, any watched share of someone else's): `MobileLiveShare` on top (the desktop `ShareTile`, plus Share volume and Full screen corners), other offers, then "In the room" / "In the call" and a row of 52 px faces. Full screen = `MobileShareFullscreen` (landscape, pinch zoom via `ZoomSurface`, controls fade). Never for your own share: `watchedShareOf` and `MobileShareFullscreen` skip `isMine`, and `MobileLiveShare` drops its tap and corner button for one.
- **The minimised call** (`MobileMinimisedCall`, B3): 56 tall, `radiusXl`, `overlay`, hairline, no shadow (it docks, never floats): in `MobileShell`'s column between the tab and the status strips (#154: floating 12 px above the nav bar it covered the last rows and the status strip's close), at the foot of a server page, under the header in `MobileChatRoute`. DM: the person's 36 avatar with their ring, the name, the mono timer in success ("Calling" while it rings), `CallMuteButton` and `CallLeaveButton` (Cancel while ringing). Room: the server's avatar, the channel, the timer, Mute, Leave through `leaveVoiceRoom` (B6: a meeting leaves or ends the meeting). The BODY opens the call (`openMobileDmCall` / `openMobileVoiceRoom`). No dragging.
- **Incoming** (`MobileIncomingCallOverlay`, mounted in `app.dart`'s mobile Stack in place of the desktop card): full screen canvas, avatar 128 (its frame kept: a ringing screen has no speaking cue), the name in `display` in their colour, "Voice call" / "Video call", the leave-the-room warning, a red Decline and a teal Accept (72, labels under). Accept answers AND opens the call screen through `hollowNavigatorKey` (B3). No countdown dial, no glow. The ringtone is `CallRingtone` (`call/call_ringtone.dart`), shared with the desktop card.
- Wakelock while video or a share is on screen; both screens close themselves (`removeRoute` when not on top) when the call or room ends.
- **The OS sees the call** (#154, wiki `os_calls`): CallKit / Telecom mirror a DM call from the moment it is answered; rooms and meetings never become system calls (Vitalik, Nov 22). While the OS holds it (a carrier call), `CallMuteButton` and the screens' mute and deafen controls ask the OS to resume instead of acting (`unlessOsHeld`); a new control of that kind does the same. Opening a call screen while held only shows it (Vitalik, Nov 21).

## A call the link ended (#154, Nov 24)

- Link labels are the real state from `LinkHealthSnapshot.label` everywhere (DM call row, person
  tile and its screen-reader mark, the phone call screen): "Unstable connection" (warning,
  `wifiLow`), "Reconnecting" (warning, `wifiOff`), "Connection lost" (error, `wifiOff`); helpers in
  `ui/call/call_link_marks.dart`. "Weak connection" is gone (swept by `call_link_labels_test`).
- A DM call that ENDS because its link gave out (`_endCallForLinkLoss` while `active`, i.e. the
  watchdog's `onGiveUp`) lingers 5 s: `lostCallProvider` (`LostCall`: callId, peer MASTER,
  seconds left, a 1 s `Timer.periodic`, never a Ticker), set BEFORE the teardown so nothing blinks.
  It is outside CallState like link health: the call is idle, no `CallStatus` guard sees it. The
  desktop `DmCallRow`, the phone `MobileCallScreen` (its idle listener skips the close while a
  linger names this peer, and closes when the linger ends) and `MobileMinimisedCall` show the
  person, "Connection lost" and a `LostCallCountdown` badge ("5 s" .. "1 s", hidden from screen
  readers). A hang-up, decline, cancel or a call that never connected closes at once; any new
  call id clears it at once (`LostCallNotifier.build` listens). Voice rooms get the labels only.
  Tests: `test/lost_call_test.dart`, `test/widget/call_link_labels_test.dart`.

## Route badge (0.14)

A quiet `HollowBadge` with a tooltip says how the media actually reaches us, read from the pair libwebrtc selected (wiki `services_voice_webrtc`, "Media route"), never guessed. `call_route.dart`: `mediaRouteLabel` (Direct, Relayed, Forwarded; both forwarder kinds read Forwarded, a forwarder leg has zero ICE servers so there is no "relayed forwarder"), `dmRouteWords`, `shareRouteWords`, `roomRouteWords` (one line for a room: the route every leg shares, else "Partly relayed" with a tooltip naming who is reached which way, each PERSON once however many devices they bring, our own other device as "your other device"), `CallRouteIndicator(DmCallRoute | RoomCallRoute)`, nothing until the first leg settles.

- Where: the desktop stage bar beside the timer (`call_stage_bar.dart`, only once the call started), `DmCallRow`, the phone DM call screen and room subtitle (`mobile_call_video_view.dart`, `mobile_voice_channel_route.dart`), a watched share's title (`share_tile.dart`). Never on the dock line (Vitalik 11-07).
- A share another member's app or the relay's forwarder passes along says Forwarded; a share our own app forwards shows nothing.
- Tight space: `components/drop_when_tight.dart` (`DropWhenTight(lead, extra)`) drops the badge WHOLE when it and the timer or title do not both fit; the timer stays. `lead` must report intrinsic widths (no LayoutBuilder inside it). The phone call bar grows with large text instead of overflowing.
- "Always relay calls" on = the next call reads Relayed.
- Tests: `test/media_route_test.dart`, `test/share_route_test.dart`, `test/widget/call_route_test.dart`, `test/widget/drop_when_tight_test.dart`; fleet `t14_route_badge_{dm,vc,mobile}.json`.

## Call record line (5.7)

LOCAL rows in a separate `call_records` table, rendered by `CallRecordRow` between messages. Full description in wiki `ui_chat_dm`, "The call record line".

## Presence elsewhere (5.5)

Dock: `VoiceQuickControls` handles DM calls too (Cancel while ringing out, else mute/deafen/Leave) and `DockIdentity`'s line reads "Calling X" / "In a call with X" (opens the DM). Classic: `VoiceChannelPanel` is the "you" block for a voice room OR a DM call (no dock there). Header: the friend chip carries `VoiceHereBadge(icon: phone)`, "in a call with you" in its label. Home: `ConversationRow.live` shows "In a call · 04:12". The incoming card (`dialogs/incoming_call_dialog.dart`) is 340 wide under the header, ghost Decline + filled Accept (Accept opens the DM on desktop), a 2 px countdown line stepped by the 1 s Timer.
